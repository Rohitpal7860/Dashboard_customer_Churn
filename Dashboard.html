Executive Summary & Findings

  • Month-to-Month Contracts Drive Churn

    42.7% of customers on month-to-month contracts leave, representing the vast majority of all churn volume.

  • Early Tenure Vulnerability

    52.9% of churn occurs within the first 6 months of joining. Onboarding requires immediate improvement.

  • Fiber Optic Service Issues

    Fiber optic users experience a surprisingly high 41.9% churn rate, suggesting potential pricing or reliability concerns.

  • High-Risk Segment Identified

    The segment of "New (0-12m) + Month-to-Month + High Charges" yields an extreme 73.5% churn rate.

Management Takeaways

1. Target First-Year Retention

Implement proactive check-ins and aggressive onboarding support in months 1-6.

2. Contract Conversion Campaigns

Offer steep discounts to convert Month-to-Month users to One-Year contracts after month 3.

3. Investigate Fiber Optic

Cross-reference Fiber Optic churn with technical support tickets and local competitor pricing.

4. Transition from Electronic Check

Incentivize automatic credit card or bank transfers, as electronic check users show 45.3% churn.
